What is Ruh?

A word that means soul.

Every biryani has a body: the rice, the meat, the masala. The soul is what happens on dum, when the handi is sealed and the flavours are left alone long enough to settle into every grain.

That patience is what we serve. Our kitchen is led by Executive Chef Raymant Raj, our masalas are crafted in-house, and every portion is weighed before it leaves the counter.

The Ruh promise

Light, never oily. Generous, never rushed. A biryani you'd happily come back for next week.
